Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a Healthcare Navigator?

A qualified healthcare professional assigned to you or your family. They coordinate doctors, hospitals, tests, reports, and follow-ups so you don't manage healthcare alone. They do not provide medical advice.

How is this different from hospital coordinators or apps?

Hospital coordinators work within one hospital. Apps provide information without accountability. Your Navigator works across providers, before, during, and after treatment.

Will I have the same Navigator every time?

Yes. You are assigned one dedicated Navigator for continuity, familiarity with your history, and faster coordination.

What does an AssistHealth membership include?

  • Dedicated Healthcare Navigator
  • Doctor & specialist coordination
  • Diagnostics & report management
  • Hospital admission & discharge support
  • Follow-ups & continuity of care
  • Family updates (including NRIs)

AssistHealth coordinates care. It does not provide medical treatment.

Does AssistHealth replace doctors or hospitals?

No. AssistHealth ensures the right care, at the right place, at the right time.

Who is AssistHealth best suited for?

  • Families with elderly parents
  • Chronic or long-term health needs
  • Busy professionals
  • NRIs managing family health in India
  • Anyone seeking organised healthcare support

What are Special Packages?

Enhanced care programs for intensive or long-term healthcare needs, including senior care, cancer care, post-surgery recovery, and complex treatments.

How are they different from regular membership?

Regular membership covers general healthcare coordination. Special Packages include higher Navigator involvement, structured care plans, and frequent follow-ups.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can upgrade anytime without repeating medical history or records.

What is AHANA?

AHANA is AssistHealth's school, corporate, and community healthcare initiative combining on-site nursing with Navigator-led coordination.

Can AHANA manage a school infirmary?

Yes. AHANA can manage the infirmary by deploying trained nurses, managing daily operations, maintaining medical records & incident logs, and coordinating parents, doctors, and hospitals.

How does this help schools and parents?

  • Reduced administrative burden
  • Standardised healthcare response
  • Better documentation & compliance
  • Clear parent communication
  • Structured escalation inside and outside campus
